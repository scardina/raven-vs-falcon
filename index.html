The Falcon's Only Path to Victory

A falcon's sole advantage lies in surprise and velocity. If a large falcon (such as a powerful Gyrfalcon) executes a stealthy, high-speed stoop from directly above and strikes the raven with a glancing or direct blow mid-air, it could incapacitate or kill it instantly.
